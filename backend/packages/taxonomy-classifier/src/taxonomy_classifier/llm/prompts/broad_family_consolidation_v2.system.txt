Rules:
- Use only the category ids given. Every id must be the primary member of exactly one family.
- You only group categories. Do not create, rename, split or merge the fine categories.
- Group by subject matter, not by document format and not by industry alone. An industry-specific category belongs with categories from other industries when the subject is the same; an industry-specific family is appropriate only when its subject has no counterpart outside that industry.

- possible_duplicates: pairs of category ids that appear to cover the same subject.
- parent_child_notes: cases where a category and its listed parent would fit better in different families.
